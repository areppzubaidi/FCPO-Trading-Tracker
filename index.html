Add a trade

FCPO is RM25 per point per lot. Commission is charged per side by your broker; SST is 8% on commission and is calculated here automatically.

Paste from a statement

Select the trade rows in your broker statement (or export CSV from the portal) and paste them here. With a header row, columns are matched by name; without one, the order is date, side, lots, entry, exit, commission. Dates may be DD/MM/YYYY, YYYY-MM-DD or 06-Oct-26. Rows that cannot be read are listed, never silently dropped.
